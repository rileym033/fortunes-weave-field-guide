      Save files & the small print

      A guide, not a spoiler.

      Your save files

      Everything you tap saves itself straight away — recruits, wishlist, supports, meals and missables. Each save file is one playthrough with its own lord.

      Keep it on your iPhone

      Open this site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The Home Screen app keeps its own storage, which iPhone doesn’t tidy away like websites you haven’t visited in a while. A connection is needed to load it.

      Move to another device

      Only needed for a new phone or browser. Copy the code here, then paste it on the other device.

      Reading the field notes

      Every lord, one roster: the Company page shows requirements for your save file’s lord. Each character’s field note also compares all four lords.

      Your journey keeps things sealed: later characters, later support conversations and later missable notes stay hidden until you set your journey (above) to the point where you reach them. Moving it back hides them again.

      Two different support measures: “Support lv.” on a card is the level a character needs with your lord to be recruited. Support conversations (C, B, A) are scenes between two characters; most open at levels 2, 3 and 4.

      Requirements, not story: unlock notes say what to do — a Part, a chapter, a path, a quest, both characters in your company — never what happens.

      Uncertainty: support data comes from a community matrix and tracker that share sources, so it isn’t independent in-game verification. Recruitment thresholds were compared between two guides; disagreements are marked in each field note. Meal preferences are community-reported.
