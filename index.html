Reading the field notes

Two different support measures: “Support lv.” is the numeric recruitment threshold. “Team supports” lists maximum conversation ranks (A/B/C), not recruitment levels and not a promise that every lower-rank scene exists. Scene availability can depend on progression or viewing order; those details are deliberately withheld.

Coverage: the same 46 NPCs as the spreadsheet, including four automatic companions and NPCs unavailable on this initial lord route. The four selectable lords are not roster rows. “No” applies to recruitment on this initial route, not later-story availability. Supports listed for unavailable NPCs are reference data, not a claim they can join this team now.

Uncertainty: “No team supports listed” means the community matrix records none with these five teammates—not independently proven absence. Rank entries were checked against the community matrix and support tracker; both share source material, so this is not independent in-game verification. Recruitment thresholds were compared between two guides. Sofia’s tutorial thresholds come from the more detailed guide. The conflicting Kiroc availability date is not asserted.

Items: quantities are explicit when available; unspecified quest-item quantities are not guessed. Item types are plain-language labels. “None listed” does not rule out quest-related costs; “—” means not applicable. Gold sorting uses the minimum stated cost and places no-listed-cost entries at zero, with not-applicable entries last.
