# Fortune's Weave Field Guide

An unofficial, spoiler-filtered companion for **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave**. Track which support conversations you've watched and what each one needs, and see who you can recruit on each Flame Lord's path. It also plans meals that help supports grow and keeps a checklist of missable scenes, paralogues and side quests.

**Use it:** https://rileym033.github.io/fortunes-weave-field-guide/
On iPhone, open it in Safari and tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

## Spoilers

Later characters, later conversations and later missables stay hidden until you set how far you've played (More → Your journey). Requirements describe *what to do*, such as a Part, a chapter, a path or both characters recruited. They never describe what happens.

## Your data

- Everything you mark is saved in your own browser on your own device. There are no accounts, no servers and no analytics, and nobody else can see or change your progress.
- To move to a new phone, use More → Move to another device to copy a transfer code.
- Clearing your browser's website data deletes your progress.
